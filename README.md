# Finance Valuation & Credit Risk Projects

Credit risk and valuation models built during my Master of Advanced Finance at Monash University.

## Amcor Credit Assessment
A bank lending credit assessment of Amcor Plc (NYSE: AMCR), evaluating a proposed term loan using:
- Five C's of Credit framework
- Risk Premium / default probability model
- Altman Z-score
- KMV model (Expected Default Frequency)

Produces a conditional loan recommendation with suggested covenant structuring.

## Bega Cheese M&A Valuation
A valuation of Woolworths Group's potential acquisition of Bega Cheese Limited, combining:
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation
- Comparable company (relative) valuation
- Post-merger synergy valuation
- Recommended deal structure (debt/equity split)

## Woodside Energy Valuation
A two-part equity valuation of Woodside Energy Group Ltd (ASX: WDS):
- **Woodside DCF Valuation.xlsx** — DuPont analysis, pro forma forecasting, cost of capital, DDM, FCF and relative valuation
- **Woodside Earnings Quality.xlsx** — earnings persistence, predictability and CFO-vs-net-profit analysis to assess the reliability of reported earnings

*All models use publicly available financial data.*
